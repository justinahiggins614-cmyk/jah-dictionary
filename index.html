Data & methodology

Original definitions. Every IWB definition is written fresh for this dictionary — all original text; no Webster's 1913 branding or text anywhere on the site. Entries marked “editors are still writing” are real headwords awaiting their authored definition.

Stamp scheme. Headwords carry JAH-DICT-W-###### stamps (alphabetical, never renumbered). Catalog terms carry JAH-DICT-T-###### stamps (reused across rebuilds, never renumbered). Either stamp pasted into the search box reopens that exact entry.

Headword vs entry vs term. A headword is an English word with IWB definitions. An entry is any item in the dictionary index. A term is a catalog-title record from the spec or patent catalogs, cross-referenced here.

Versioning. Definitions carry a definition version; stamps are permanent IDs; every field on an entry recomputes from fixed public rules (press “Verify record” on any entry to recompute in the browser).

Machine access. api.json (bot manifest) · stats.json (counts) · headwords.csv (word,pos,stamp — 102,218 rows) · sitemap-index.xml (headword deep links + A–Z pages; catalog terms are sitemapped by their home catalogs). Each entry also serves JSON-LD (schema.org DefinedTerm) and a canonical ?w= URL.
