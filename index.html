Data & methodology

Original definitions. Every Signature Dictionary definition is written fresh for this dictionary — all original text; no Webster's 1913 branding or text anywhere on the site. Entries marked “editors are still writing” are real headwords awaiting their authored definition.

Stamp scheme. Headwords carry JAH-DICT-W-###### stamps (alphabetical, never renumbered). Catalog terms carry JAH-DICT-T-###### stamps (reused across rebuilds, never renumbered). Either stamp pasted into the search box reopens that exact entry. A catalog record ID (JAH-SPEC-###### or a patent publication number) pasted into search resolves to its catalog-term entry.

Headword vs entry vs term. A headword is an English word with Signature Dictionary definitions. An entry is any item in the dictionary index. A term is a catalog-title record from the spec or patent catalogs, cross-referenced here.

Versioning. Definitions carry a definition version; stamps are permanent IDs; every field on an entry recomputes from fixed public rules (press “Verify record” on any entry to recompute in the browser).

Machine access. api.json (bot manifest) · stats.json (counts) · headwords.csv (word,pos,stamp — 102,218 rows) · sitemap-index.xml (headword deep links + A–Z pages; catalog terms are sitemapped by their home catalogs). Each entry also serves JSON-LD (schema.org DefinedTerm) and a canonical ?w= URL.

Welcome to The Signature Dictionary

Every English word, defined two ways — the normal authored definition and the JAH deterministic definition. Here’s how to use it:

  1. Search any word. Type a word in the search box up top and press Define — an exact headword always wins. Paste a JAH-DICT-W-###### stamp to reopen an entry.
  2. Read the entry. Numbered senses, phonetic respelling, word history, usage examples, related words & opposites, and the deterministic definition — all on one page.
  3. Use the entry tools. Read aloud, Copy, Download, Verify record, and the runnable Python word program (Copy, Download, Run demo).
  4. Ask the AI teacher. Every entry has a personal AI teacher at the bottom — ask it questions about the word you’re reading.
  5. Try the Finder or a random word. Describe a word in plain words and press Find it, or jump to a Random word from the header.

? How to use this site

Every feature on The Signature Dictionary, in plain words.

🔍 Word search

  • Type any word in the search box at the top and press Define; suggestions appear as you type.
  • An exact headword always wins, however you type it — “thumb-drive” matches “thumb drive”. Typing is forgiving about case, hyphens, and spaces.
  • Your query stays in the ?q= URL, so a search is shareable and re-editable.

🎫 Stamp & catalog record-ID search

  • Paste a JAH-DICT-W-###### (headword) or JAH-DICT-T-###### (catalog term) stamp into search to reopen that exact entry.
  • Paste a catalog record ID — JAH-SPEC-######, JAH-WORD-######, or a patent publication number — to jump to its catalog-term entry. You land on the stable ?id= link.
  • Every entry shows its stamp; pasting the stamp reopens that entry.

❓ When a word isn't found

  • The page offers “Did you mean” suggestions, “Starting with…” prefix links, and Nearby headwords automatically.
  • A “Surprise me: random word” link is offered too.

🗃 Entry-type filter

  • On search results, the Show: dropdown filters to authored definitions, template-derived definitions, pending definitions, or catalog terms.
  • The filter stays in the URL (?filter=), so filtered results are shareable. (The legacy ?defined=1 link still works.)

🔀 Random word

  • The Random word link in the header opens a real random headword — never blank, and never the same word twice in a row.
  • It lands on the stable ?w= deep link, so you can bookmark it.

📅 Word of the day

  • One featured headword per day, deterministic from the date — everyone sees the same word all day.
  • Tap the word (or “Open the full entry →”) to read its full entry.

🧭 The Finder

  • THE FINDER — dictionary assistance: describe the word you're thinking of in plain words (“a word for a noisy argument”) and press Find it.
  • It matches your keywords against the dictionary only — it never invents words.

🔢 Browse A–Z

  • The letter row browses every headword starting with a letter, with sub-letter paging inside long lists.
  • Static A–Z pages also work with no JavaScript.

📖 The entry page

  • Every headword opens with its phonetic respelling right under the title, its part of speech, and numbered senses of the normal Signature Dictionary definition.
  • The heading carries a provenance badge: AUTHORED (written by the editors), TEMPLATE-DERIVED (produced by deterministic rules), or PENDING DEFINITION (a real headword whose authored definition the editors are still writing). Very long entries open in a scrollable panel so the page stays fast.
  • The Entry ID line tells you the entry's permanent identity: paste that ID into search to reopen the entry.

🔊 Pronunciation

  • The Pronunciation section gives the IWB phonetic respelling — an approximate editor-written guide, not IPA — with a 🔊 Hear word button.
  • The same phonetic respelling appears under the headword and on search results.

📜 Word history

  • The Word history section explains how the word is formed, and names its source: derived from the dictionary's own word analysis or composed by the Signature Dictionary editors.
  • The editors never present a composed history as established historical fact.

📰 Description · 💬 Usage examples · 🔗 Related words & opposites · 📋 Word forms

  • Description: full-sentence worded descriptions of each sense.
  • Usage examples: editor-composed example sentences.
  • Related words & opposites: the word's family (words built from the same base inside this dictionary) plus opposites. Every related word is a link.
  • Word forms: inflections with dictionary-validated spellings.

🔢 JAH deterministic definition

  • A machine-computed reading of the word: letter values, alphabetic sum, digital root, FNV-1a and djb2 signatures, letter counts, estimated syllables, Scrabble value, resonance class, and entry hash.
  • Fixed public rules — the same word verifies identically on any machine, any year.

🏷 Official deterministic stamp

  • Every entry carries a permanent JAH-DICT-W-###### (headword) or JAH-DICT-T-###### (catalog term) stamp. Stamps are never renumbered.
  • Paste the stamp into the search box to reopen that entry.

💻 Word program

  • Every headword carries a real, runnable Python 3 word program that recomputes the deterministic signature from fixed public rules — stdlib only, no network, no file writes, identical output on any machine.
  • 📋 Copy program copies it, ⬇ Download .py saves it, and ▶ Run demo executes the same algorithm right in the page.
  • The “What the word program does” panel above the buttons explains the contract in plain words.

🎓 AI teacher

  • Every entry has a personal AI Teacher at the bottom. It reads the canonical entry above and answers your questions in plain words — it can never change or revise the entry.
  • Type in the box and press Ask. If the answer can't be delivered, a visible error message says so.

❓ Ask this entry

  • The ASK THIS ENTRY box on every entry answers four quick questions straight from the record: what it means, how to say it, where it comes from, and show-me examples.

🔊 Read aloud

  • 🔊 Read aloud speaks the entry through a tiered voice engine (ResponsiveVoice first, then Google TTS hosts).
  • ⏸ Pause / ⏫ Resume pauses the current chunk in place and resumes it — you never lose your spot. 🔊 Hear word speaks just the headword. The speed control changes the voice.
  • Playback uses audio elements, so it works where device speech synthesis is unavailable.

⧉ Copy · ⬇ Download

  • The entry toolbar offers 📋 Copy entry, ⬇ Download .txt, ⬇ Download JSON, and ⬇ Download CSV — the full entry in your choice of format.
  • The record panel under the headword adds OPEN (the entry's own link), SHARE (copies/shares the stable ?id= link), COPY, DOWNLOAD, and READ ALOUD.
  • The word-program toolbar separately offers Copy program, Download .py, and Run demo.

✓ Verify record

  • ✓ Verify record recomputes the entry's four signatures (stamp format, FNV-1a, djb2, entry hash) right in your browser.
  • ✓ means the record is byte-identical to the shipped dictionary.

⚠ Report an issue

  • ⚠ Report an issue files a local-only report with a permanent JAH-DICT-R-###### ID, saved on your device — copy or export it for editorial review.
  • Reports never edit the dictionary: corrections arrive as new definition versions, never silent replacements.

⚙ Word patent · 🗃 In the JAH catalogs · 🗺 The JAH Web

  • ⚙ Word patent → opens the word's JAH-WORD record in the Spec Catalog — a Signature DRAFT spec: a draft ready to file, not filed and not a granted patent.
  • In the JAH catalogs shows how many spec and patent titles use the word, with one-tap search links into JAH Wiki. Catalog-term entries instead link to their home catalog (Spec Catalog or Patent Catalog), plus the JAH Wiki article and Wiki Leaks dossier.
  • The JAH Web puts every Signature site one tap away. Also filed under this headword lists other entries sharing the same title; Nearby headwords walks the dictionary alphabetically.

◑ Dark mode

  • The ◑ button toggles dark mode. Your choice is remembered on this device.
